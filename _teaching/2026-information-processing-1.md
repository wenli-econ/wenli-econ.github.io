**Language:** Japanese  
**Semester:** Spring Semester  

This course introduces practical data-processing skills for economics and business students. The first part focuses on Excel-based data handling, formulas, functions, charts, and basic economic data exercises. The second part introduces the basics of R and RStudio for data analysis.

## Tools & Materials

Excel, R, RStudio, Moodle-based exercises

## Topics

Spreadsheet operations; data cleaning; functions and formulas; visualization; introductory R programming; basic statistical analysis
