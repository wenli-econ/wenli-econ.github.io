**Language:** Japanese  
**Format:** Intensive course  

This course introduces core ideas in social informatics, focusing on how data, algorithms, and digital technologies shape society and economic behavior. The course emphasizes data interpretation, empirical thinking, and the social implications of digital technologies rather than advanced programming.

## Topics

Social data; data ethics; visualization; sampling; bias in data; causal inference; machine learning; AI and society
