**Language:** Japanese and English  

This seminar combines reading-based discussion and introductory empirical analysis. Students read economics-related texts, prepare summaries, discuss social and economic issues, and later learn the basics of data analysis using R.

## Topics

Reading and discussion; research presentation; empirical thinking; introductory R; data analysis in economics
